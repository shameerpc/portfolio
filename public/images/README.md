# Asset Guide — Portfolio

Drop your real files into these folders. Components already reference these paths.

## images/about/
- `profile.jpg` — Your professional headshot or portrait

## images/creative/posters/
- `poster-01.jpg` through `poster-12.jpg`
  Category will be "Posters"

## images/creative/social-media/
- `social-01.jpg` through `social-12.jpg`
  Category will be "Social Media"

## images/creative/advertising/
- `ad-01.jpg` through `ad-12.jpg`
  Category will be "Advertising"

## images/creative/brochures/
- `brochure-thumb-01.jpg` through `brochure-thumb-12.jpg`
  Category will be "Brochures"

## images/webdev/
- `project-01.jpg` through `project-06.jpg`
  Screenshots of your web projects

## images/brochures/
- `brochure-01.jpg` through `brochure-06.jpg`
  Full brochure preview images

## videos/
- `showreel.mp4` — Your showreel / demo reel video
- `showreel-poster.jpg` — Thumbnail for the video player
